PROMPT A — STYLE (paste first, as its own screen or theme prompt)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A website for "S&A Freshlevering", a Dutch fresh fruit and vegetable delivery
company serving restaurants and households. All UI copy in Dutch.

Style: clean, fresh, trustworthy, food-market. Lots of white space, generous
padding, rounded corners at 14px, very soft shadows. Feels premium but
approachable — a good local greengrocer, not a supermarket chain.

Colors: deep forest green #1B4D2E for headers, footers and primary buttons.
Fresh leaf green #4CAF2F for accents, highlights and active states. Off-white
#F7F9F5 page background. White #FFFFFF cards. Charcoal #1F2421 body text.
WhatsApp green #25D366 only on WhatsApp buttons.

Typography: serif headings (Lora), clean sans body text (Inter). Headings are
large and confident. Body text is comfortable, line height 1.6.

Recurring graphic: a soft organic curved swoosh divider in deep green with a
thin lime-green outline, used to separate the hero from the content and above
the footer. Maximum two per page.

Photography: bright high-key close-up produce — lettuce, green apples, kiwi,
grapes, lime, broccoli, carrots in a wooden crate — on a white background in
natural daylight, shallow depth of field. Never dark or moody.

Logo in the header: a serif "S&A" monogram with a small green leaf sprout
above the ampersand, with "FRESHLEVERING" underneath in condensed uppercase
letters and the small wide-spaced tagline "BEZORGEN VAN GROENTEN EN FRUIT".
Never render the logo narrower than about 160px — the serif monogram and the
condensed sans wordmark stop reading as one lockup below that.

Avoid: gradients, heavy shadows, oversized empty areas, decorative
illustrations, generic startup or SaaS aesthetics, e-commerce grid layouts,
inconsistent corner radii, inconsistent button styles, dark food photography.
The target feeling is a premium local greengrocer that also serves
professional kitchens.

PROMPT E — FULL BUILD
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Build a two-page website for "S&A Freshlevering", a Dutch fresh fruit and
vegetable delivery company. It serves restaurants and caterers first and
consumers second. All UI copy in Dutch. Mobile-first and responsive.

Stack: static HTML + CSS + vanilla JavaScript, no build step and no runtime
dependencies. If I ask for React instead, use React + Tailwind and keep the
same behaviour.

Deliver: index.html, prijslijst.html, prijslijst-template.csv, SETUP.md, and
one runnable self-test.

If design screenshots or a Figma export are attached, they are the visual
source of truth — match typography, spacing, proportions, colour and component
geometry closely. Deviate only where accessibility, usability or responsive
behaviour requires it, and say so when you do.

── BRAND ──
Deep forest green #1B4D2E (headers, footers, primary buttons), fresh leaf
green #4CAF2F (accents and active states), off-white #F7F9F5 page background,
white #FFFFFF cards, charcoal #1F2421 body text, WhatsApp green #25D366 only
on WhatsApp buttons. Serif headings (Lora), sans body (Inter), line-height 1.6.
Rounded corners 14px, soft shadows, generous white space. A soft organic curved
green "swoosh" divider appears at most twice per page — below the hero and
above the footer. Produce photography is bright, high-key, white-background,
natural daylight. Never dark or moody.
Logo: serif "S&A" monogram with a small leaf sprout above the ampersand,
"FRESHLEVERING" in condensed uppercase underneath, tagline "BEZORGEN VAN
GROENTEN EN FRUIT" in small wide-tracked caps.

── PAGE 1: index.html ──
1. Sticky white header: logo left; nav "Prijslijst / Hoe het werkt / Zakelijk /
   Contact"; right a dark green phone pill "06 83 41 56 76" and a round
   WhatsApp button. On mobile: logo + one WhatsApp CTA.
2. Split hero. Left: H1 "Verse groenten en fruit, morgenochtend in uw keuken",
   a sub-line, and an outlined clock badge "Voor 22:00 besteld = volgende
   ochtend geleverd". Buttons: solid "Bekijk prijslijst", outlined "Bestel via
   WhatsApp". Right: produce photo bleeding off the right edge, its left edge
   cut by the swoosh. Below: trust row — Betrouwbaar (shield), Snel (truck),
   Gratis bezorgd tot in uw keuken (house).
3. Two large audience cards side by side — the main fork of the page.
   "Voor horeca en bedrijven" (dark green, white text): Prijzen exclusief BTW /
   Bulkverpakkingen / Vaste bezorgdag / Factuur op rekening → links to
   prijslijst.html?mode=b2b.
   "Voor particulieren" (white, green border): Prijzen inclusief BTW / Geen
   minimum bestelling / Bestel via WhatsApp → prijslijst.html?mode=b2c.
4. "Hoe het werkt": three numbered steps with line icons — "Stel je lijst
   samen of app je bestelling" / "Wij halen het 's ochtends vers op de veiling"
   / "Voor 22:00 besteld, volgende ochtend geleverd".
5. "Ons assortiment": six category tiles (Groenten, Fruit, Aardappelen & uien,
   Kruiden & cressen, Champignons, Gesneden groenten) linking into the filtered
   price list.
6. Full-width dark green band with a live countdown to today's 22:00 cutoff:
   "Nog 3u 12m om voor morgenochtend te bestellen" and a white CTA. Compute
   the remaining time from the visitor's current local time and tick it down —
   never hard-code the figure. After 22:00 it switches to static copy naming
   the next delivery cycle instead of counting to a deadline that has passed.
7. Three testimonials plus a three-stat row. Mark this copy TODO — do not
   invent customers or numbers.
8. Bezorggebied: a plain list of served towns, marked TODO.
9. Dark green footer above a swoosh: logo, phone, WhatsApp, www.freshlevering.nl,
   info@freshlevering.nl, bezorgtijden, KvK and BTW placeholders, and a
   handwritten script "Betrouwbaar en snel".
10. Mobile-only fixed bottom bar: [Bel] | [WhatsApp].

── PAGE 2: prijslijst.html — the core of the site ──
About 100 products in 14 categories. It must feel like a fast catalogue, not
a webshop. No cart, no checkout, no payments, no accounts.

Layout: dark green page header ("Prijslijst" + subtitle), then a sticky control
bar containing a search field ("Zoek product, bijv. tomaat…"), a segmented
toggle "Zakelijk | Particulier", a horizontally scrollable row of category
chips with "Alles" first, and a meta line "104 producten · prijzen bijgewerkt
op <datum>".
Below that, products grouped under serif category headings with a lime
underline. Each product is a white rounded card row: name in bold with the unit
in smaller grey text underneath, the price right-aligned in bold tabular
figures with a tiny "ex. BTW" / "incl. BTW" caption under it, and a round green
plus button.
Below the list, a light green terms panel whose text switches with the mode,
and a small "Download prijslijst (PDF)" link.

Price states, each visually distinct and each labelled in text, never by colour
alone:
- normal price
- "Dagprijs" pill when prijs_ex is blank or status is dagprijs (market-priced
  items like asperges or cantharellen). Never render an empty cell.
- "Seizoen" pill next to the name for seasonal items
- "Uitverkocht" pill in muted red, row greyed, plus button removed, and the
  item blocked from being added at all — not merely missing its button

── DATA: GOOGLE SHEETS ──
The client edits a Google Sheet and the site follows. No CMS, no deploy, no
developer needed for a price change.

  Columns: categorie | product | eenheid | prijs_ex | btw | status | b2c
  prijs_ex  price EXCLUDING BTW, dot decimal (1.95). Blank = dagprijs.
  btw       optional. Blank means 9, the Dutch rate for all fresh produce.
            S&A sells produce only, so in practice this column stays empty.
  status    normaal | dagprijs | seizoen | uitverkocht
  b2c       TRUE if the pack size suits a consumer, FALSE = wholesale only
  Row order in the sheet IS the display order. Do not add a sort column.

Read a published-to-web CSV URL from a single SHEET_CSV_URL constant at the top
of the script, fetch it at runtime with a ~10 minute cache, and keep a baked-in
copy of the last known list as a fallback so a Sheets outage never blanks the
page. Do not fetch at build time — produce prices move daily and a stale price
generates complaints. Write a quote-aware CSV parser; product names contain
commas and quotes. Skip rows whose first cell starts with "#". On fetch failure
show "Prijslijst tijdelijk niet beschikbaar. Bel of app 06 83 41 56 76" — never
a blank table.

── BTW: ONE PRICE, TWO VIEWS ──
The sheet stores ex-BTW only. The Particulier view computes incl-BTW at render
time as prijs_ex × (1 + btw/100) rounded to cents. Never store two price
columns. Every price on screen carries an explicit "ex. BTW" or "incl. BTW"
caption — an unlabelled ex-BTW price shown to a consumer is a legal problem in
the Netherlands, not a styling nitpick. The Particulier view also hides every
row where b2c is FALSE (25kg baal, 12.5kg doos, 5kg boter): hidden, not deleted.

── BEHAVIOUR ──
- Search and category filtering are client-side, instant and case-insensitive.
  No network request per keystroke. Filtering preserves sheet row order.
- Mode resolves in this precedence: an explicit ?mode=b2b / ?mode=b2c in the
  URL wins, then the value saved in localStorage, then the default Zakelijk.
  Switching mode updates prices, BTW captions, visible rows and the terms panel
  without a page reload, and a returning restaurant never re-picks Zakelijk.
- The plus button turns that row into a −/quantity/+ stepper. Quantities persist
  in localStorage.
- Once anything is selected, a fixed dark green bottom bar appears: "7 artikelen
  in je lijst", a "Wissen" button, and a WhatsApp CTA opening a wa.me link to
  31683415676 with a prefilled Dutch message listing quantity, product and unit,
  followed by empty "Bezorgadres:" and "Gewenste bezorgdag:" lines.
- Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast, semantic HTML, correct heading order, full
  keyboard navigation, visible focus rings, 44px minimum tap targets,
  aria-pressed on the toggle and chips, aria-live on the result list, alt text
  on every produce photo, respects prefers-reduced-motion. No state is ever
  communicated by colour alone.
- Performance: no carousel, no heavy libraries, lazy-load images below the fold,
  target LCP under 2.5s on 4G.
- Motion: fade-and-rise on scroll into view (200ms) and a hover lift on cards.
  Nothing parallax, nothing auto-playing.

── ALSO DELIVER ──
- prijslijst-template.csv: the seed sheet with the exact columns above, ~100
  sample rows covering every category and every status value, and both b2c
  TRUE and FALSE pack sizes.
- SETUP.md, in Dutch, for a non-technical client: import the CSV into a Sheet,
  replace the sample rows with real products, Bestand > Delen > Publiceren op
  internet > tab > CSV, copy the URL, paste it into SHEET_CSV_URL, open
  index.html. Note that prices go in ex BTW and that a blank price means
  dagprijs.
- One runnable self-test — plain asserts, no test framework — covering:
  CSV quoted commas, CSV escaped quotes, comment rows skipped, BTW rounding at
  9% and at an explicit 21%, blank btw defaulting to 9, blank prijs_ex yielding
  dagprijs, b2c filtering hiding wholesale
  rows in Particulier mode, an uitverkocht item refusing to be added, quantity
  round-tripping through localStorage, and the generated WhatsApp message body.
  It must fail loudly and name what broke.

── HOUSEKEEPING ──
Keep the BTW calculation in one function, the mode logic in one place and the
order state in one place — that is what stops this drifting, not file count.
No dead code, no lorem ipsum, no dependency added for something a few lines do.

Real Dutch copy throughout. Placeholder produce images with descriptive alt
text. Never invent customer names, review quotes, statistics, delivery towns,
postcodes, KvK or BTW numbers — mark every one of those TODO.

── DONE MEANS ──
Both pages render on desktop and mobile, the CSV loads at runtime with the
fallback working when it does not, the self-test passes, and every visible
price carries a BTW label. Report honestly what you verified by running it and
what you only eyeballed — do not claim a check you did not execute.
